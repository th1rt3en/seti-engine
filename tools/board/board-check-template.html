How to read the drawings

Each wheel is drawn as 8 slots around a circle, numbered 0 to 7. Slot 0 starts at the 3 o'clock direction of the picture and the numbers go counter-clockwise, the same way the discs turn. Rings are numbered from the Sun outward: ring 1 is the innermost.
