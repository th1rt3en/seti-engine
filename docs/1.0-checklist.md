# 1.0 checklist

Drawn up 2026-10-19 from `docs/PLAN.md` (phase 6, section 5 gates, section 7 risks), `docs/spec/`, `docs/asset-gaps.md` and the state of the code. The plan itself has no checklist: this file is the working list. Tick an item when it is done and checked; add a date and a note. Items marked *(proposal)* are the agent's suggestions and are not yet approved by the owner.

## A. Rules fidelity

The table is in `docs/spec/untested-rules.md`.
- [ ] A review agent has checked the rules changes of the expansion against the rulebook text (CLAUDE.md, Conventions).
- [ ] Every entry of `docs/spec/ambiguities.md` is ruled and copied into the spec. Check the table for open entries; A-001 still has an open part (what orbiting or landing on the SE EN 01 planet costs and gives).
- [x] Owner check of the expansion data (`docs/spec/expansion-data.md`): the owner reports it as already checked (2026-10-19). The spec still says "owner check pending"; update that wording when the spec is next edited.

- [ ] Rulebook worked examples are scripted scenarios; the random-play fuzzers run for 1 to 4 players with and without the expansion; replay equals live state (re-run before each release).

## F. UI improvements

Mark each as *wanted* or *not wanted* and the agent works from the wanted ones. Items marked *(proposal)* are the agent's suggestions; the others come from gaps seen in the code and in play.

### F1. Known gaps
- [x] The Mascamite sample spaces are places on the species board with their tokens (A-056 ruled by the owner, 2026-10-19); to be seen in a live game.
- [ ] The places that were measured by eye (`speciesSpaces.ts`, `PlayerBoard.tsx`, the Amoeba and Arkhos boards, the Exertian prompt circles, the organelle tokens) are re-checked on several screen sizes and on a high-density screen.
- [ ] Audit every decision kind against `buildSpots` (`targets.ts`): any choice that has a place on a picture is a highlighted place, and only the rest is a button of the bar.
- [ ] One look for every confirm and cancel: the buttons over a card, a tech, a display card, the deck, a board place and an alien card are one component with one style, and the setting that turns confirmations off applies to all of them.
- [ ] Log: every gain has its own line with its source. Audit the lines that are still merged (rival turns, glyphs, organelles, the signals of a scan, the end of the round) and the lines that name a hidden card.
- [ ] The tracker, the player boards and the missions panel show the same numbers as the engine in every phase (income, tucked cards, end-of-game cards, danger, signal tokens); a test compares them for a few saved games.
- [ ] Every decision has a clear text in the context bar that names its source in parentheses (done for most; audit the rest), and says why a choice is empty (no organelle, no probe to move, no card to take) with a skip button.

### F2. Phone and tablet
- [ ] Every panel and dock is checked at 360 and 390 px wide and in landscape: the player boards, the species boards, the tracker, the log, the display, the hand and the context bar.
- [ ] Touch targets on the board are at least 44 px and the highlighted places do not overlap; a place that is hard to hit gets a larger invisible target.
- [ ] Pinch zoom and pan on the board are smooth, a double tap does not click a place, and the board keeps its zoom while the page is open.
- [ ] The page keeps its place when the phone is rotated, when the keyboard opens and when the tab goes to the background and comes back.
- [ ] The hand, the display and the log do not hide the board when open: a clear way to fold all docks at once.

### F3. Clarity and help
- [ ] *(wanted)* A short "what can I do now" help for each decision, behind the ? button of the context bar, with a link to the rule text.
- [ ] *(wanted)* A "last move" highlight on the board (the place or piece the opponent just changed), also after a reconnect or a refresh.
- [ ] *(wanted)* A "what changed since my last turn" summary when it becomes the player's turn in a turn-based game: the moves of the opponents in one list.
- [ ] *(wanted)* A preview of the result of a move before it is confirmed: the score, the resources and the tracks after it (for a launch, a landing, a research, a mission).
- [x] *(skipped by the owner, 2026-10-19)* A rules lookup: a page and a search box with the spec text of each rule and each card effect, opened from a card, a tech or a log line.
- [ ] *(wanted)* Tooltips for the icons of the tracker, the log and the cards (what the symbol means, in words).
- [ ] *(wanted)* A first-game tutorial or guided tour for new players, and a short rules summary for each phase of a turn.
- [x] *(wanted)* A legend of the log: the icons and the words of its lines. (built and deployed 2026-10-19; not tried in a live game)

### F4. Log and replay
- [x] *(wanted)* A filter of the log (mine, the rival, the opponents, gains only, one source) and a search. (built and deployed 2026-10-19; not tried in a live game)
- [ ] *(wanted)* Clicking a log line shows the board as it was after that move, without leaving the live game.
- [x] *(wanted)* The replay viewer: step forward and back, play and pause, jump to a round, see the log line of each step, and share a link to a move. (built and deployed 2026-10-19; not tried in a live game)
- [x] *(wanted)* The log can be copied or downloaded as text for a bug report or a discussion. (built and deployed 2026-10-19; not tried in a live game)
- [ ] The log keeps its place when new lines arrive while the player has scrolled up, with a "new moves" button; the size the player set (desktop) is kept.

### F5. Turn flow and feedback
- [x] *(wanted)* A turn notification: the page title and a sound when it becomes the player's turn (the sound setting exists), and an optional browser notification for turn-based games. (built and deployed 2026-10-19; not tried in a live game)
- [ ] *(wanted)* Short animations for the main events (a probe moving, a signal, a card going to the hand, a rotation), with a setting to turn them off and respect for "reduce motion".
- [x] *(wanted)* A visible clock for each player with a warning in the last minute; a clear "waiting for [name]" state with the reason the engine waits. (built and deployed 2026-10-19; not tried in a live game)
- [x] *(wanted)* Undo and the take-back of a trade (D31, D32) shown as one clear control that says what will be taken back. (built and deployed 2026-10-19; not tried in a live game)
- [x] *(wanted)* Keyboard shortcuts for the common actions (confirm, cancel, end turn, undo, fold a dock) and a list of them. (built and deployed 2026-10-19; not tried in a live game)
- [x] *(skipped by the owner, 2026-10-19)* A guard against a mis-click on the final steps: ending the turn with a main action left, or with a free card still to take.

### F6. Cards and boards
- [ ] *(wanted)* A larger card view (hover or long press) that shows the card text as text next to the picture, for the cards whose print is small.
- [x] *(skipped by the owner, 2026-10-19)* Cards in the hand can be sorted and grouped (by cost, by sector colour, by type) and the order is remembered.
- [ ] *(wanted)* A tech and gold tile overview that shows who has what and what is still open, and a running estimate of the end-of-game cards and gold tiles (the player's own, labelled as an estimate).
- [ ] *(wanted)* Highlight the sectors, planets and probes that a hovered card or mission refers to.
- [ ] The player boards panel shows the other players' boards read-only and at the same size as the player's own.
- [ ] The solo rival: its board, deck and action cards are as readable as a player's.

### F7. Settings and accessibility
- [x] *(skipped by the owner, 2026-10-19)* Colour-blind friendly player colours (shapes or patterns on the pieces) and a high-contrast mode.
- [ ] *(wanted)* A check with a screen reader of the lobby, the settings, the context bar and the log; a logical focus order and visible focus rings on every interactive element, including the places on the board.
- [x] *(skipped by the owner, 2026-10-19)* A text size setting that applies to the whole page (the hand size setting exists); a dark and a light theme.
- [ ] *(wanted)* Settings are saved with the account, not only in the browser, and follow the player to another device.
- [ ] Respect "reduce motion" and "prefers contrast" in every animation and highlight.
