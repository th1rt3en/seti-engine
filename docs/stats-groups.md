# Groups of the sources of each resource (2026-10-26, for the owner's review)

The pies of "where it came from" show groups of sources. The credits are the owner's choice; the others were made from what really gave each resource in the 58
finished games that the engine can replay (every gain of every player, the solo rival's included), and are open to review. `gain.<kind>.<resource>` is the
source (see `docs/stats-sources.md`); what the actions and free actions gave is told by action (`gainmove.<action>.<resource>`). The Others group lists what it
holds when it is pointed at.

| Resource | Groups |
|---|---|
| **Credits** | Income (the round, and the credit of a tucked card) · Organization · Alien effects (species boards, traces, Arkhos exploration, glyphs, anomaly tokens, Mascamite samples and their delivery) · Missions · Quick start cards · Technologies · Others |
| **Energy** | the same, and **Planets** (moons give energy) |
| **Cards** | the same, and **Planets** (orbiting) · **Passing** (the end-of-round card) · **Trading and buying cards** |
| **Data** | the same, and **Marking signals** (the largest) · **Planets** (the first lander's data, orbiting, moons) · **Playing cards** · **Free corners** |
| **Publicity** | the same, and **Moving probes** (comets, asteroids, planets) · **Placing data in the computer** (the second space) · **Completing sectors** · **Planets** · **Playing cards** · **Free corners** |
| **Points** | the same, and **Planets** (the largest after traces) · **Marking signals** · **Completing sectors** · **Playing cards** · **Free corners** · **Passing** · **Placing data in the computer** (the upper space, 2 points) · the end-of-game parts |
| **Movement** (new) | Energy turned into movement · Playing cards · Free corners · Technologies (the Scan Booster) · Organization · Missions · Alien effects · Others |
| **Exofossils** | Alien effects (the traces) · Planets · Completing sectors ('Oumuamua's tile) · Playing cards · Others |

What each resource was spent on is a separate pie (see `docs/stats-sources.md`); for movement it is "Moving probes".
