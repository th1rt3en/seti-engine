# Solo game

Source: base rulebook pp. 22 to 27 and the alien rule sheets (solo rules sections). The rival's action cards and their action procedures are in `solo-rival-cards.md` (SOLO-RIVAL-01 to 11); this file covers setup, progress, objectives and the end of the game.

## Setup

- **SOLO-01** (p.22) Set up the game as for two players (SETUP-10 to 21 with 2 players, including 2 neutral markers at each neutral milestone).
- **SOLO-02** (p.22) The rival takes all the pieces of one color. One of the rival's markers goes on the starting space of the progress track.
- **SOLO-03** (p.22) The starting player is determined at random. The starting player marker moves between the player and the rival after each round. The first player starts with 1 point and the other with 2. Both publicity markers start at 4.
- **SOLO-04** (p.22) Difficulty: choose one of 5 levels, shown by the gold stars on the back of each player board. The rival uses the player board of the chosen difficulty. 1-star and 2-star share a board. The rival's board shows: the progress track and the growth icon, the objective stack composition, and the starting progress marker space.
- **SOLO-05** (p.22) The rival's action deck: take the 4 basic action cards (S.1 to S.4), shuffle them together face down. On 3-star difficulty or higher, randomly select 1 advanced action card, shuffle it into the deck. The rest of the advanced cards are kept nearby: the rival gains them during the game (SOLO-12). Each alien species' special action card is set aside (SOLO-RIVAL-09).
- **SOLO-06** (p.22) Rulebook says the advanced action cards are "S.5 to S.15". The card art shows S.15 is the Mascamites species card, so the advanced cards are S.5 to S.14. Open question A-010.

## Objectives (difficulty 2 stars and up; skip on 1 star)

- **SOLO-OBJ-01** (p.22) Objectives come in 3 levels (I, II, III). Shuffle the objectives of each level face down separately.
- **SOLO-OBJ-02** (p.22) As indicated by the rival's player board, take the matching number of objectives of each level and assemble the stack: level III at the bottom, level II in the middle, level I on top. The rest are returned to the box. Example, 4 stars: 2 level I, 6 level II, 7 level III. The numbers per difficulty are on the board (data: SOLO-DATA-01).
- **SOLO-OBJ-03** (p.22) After assembling the stack, reveal the top 3 tiles. These are the player's first objectives.
- **SOLO-OBJ-04** (p.26, p.27) Each objective has one or more tasks. When a task is done, mark it; when all tasks are marked, the objective is completed. A single trigger can mark only one task, even across different objectives (the player chooses). Tasks using triggerable-mission symbols are marked when the depicted thing is done. A task with a slash "/" is marked when either part is done. Task types explained: reach 16 points (mark immediately if already there); have at least 5 data tokens in the data pool (tokens in the computer do not count; mark immediately if already there); reach 9 publicity (mark immediately if already there); visit asteroids; visit a comet (visits must happen after the task is revealed); play a card costing exactly 3 credits (as an effect, not a free action); complete a mission (after the task is revealed). Other tasks are shown by symbols (data: SOLO-DATA-02).
- **SOLO-OBJ-05** (p.26) At the end of each turn, completed objectives move into a completed-objectives pile and are replaced with new tiles from the stack, so the player always has 3 objectives to work on until the stack runs out.
- **SOLO-OBJ-06** (p.26) At the end of rounds 1, 2, 3 and 4, before getting income, the player spends completed objectives equal to the round number just ended (1, 2, 3, 4 tiles removed from the pile). For each objective the player cannot spend, the rival advances 3 spaces on the progress track.
- **SOLO-OBJ-07** (p.26, p.27) At the end of round 5, count all uncompleted objectives (those in the row of three and those still in the stack): the rival scores 5 points for each.
- **SOLO-OBJ-08** (p.26) On 1-star difficulty there are no objectives: the rival does not progress for uncompleted objectives between rounds and does not gain points for them at the end of the game.

## The rival

- **SOLO-10** (p.23) The rival does not keep track of credits, energy and cards. Whenever an effect gives the rival one of these, advance the progress marker 1 space for each unit gained. The cards can come from anywhere (card row, tucked-income effect, the card gained from passing, helping to discover an alien species).
- **SOLO-11** (p.23) When the rival gains the tuck-a-card-for-income effect, it advances 4 spaces on the progress track instead. This usually happens when the rival places an orbiter.
- **SOLO-12** (p.23) Whenever the progress marker crosses the growth icon, add 1 random advanced action card face down to the top of the rival's action deck. It will come up on the rival's next turn (unless the rival has already passed this round). It is permanently part of the deck. The base rulebook does not say what happens if no advanced action cards are left to add: open A-012.
- **SOLO-13** (p.23) Every action that shows the progress icon makes the rival advance 1 space. The rival always moves clockwise on the progress track.
- **SOLO-14** (p.23) Rival points: whenever the rival gains victory points, move its score counter. The rival triggers neutral and gold milestones like a player.
- **SOLO-15** (p.23) Gold tiles: the rival always claims only the first, most valuable spot on a gold tile. If several gold tiles have their first slot available, it chooses the leftmost or the rightmost by the decision arrow of the current action card. If all first spots are taken, it claims nothing.
- **SOLO-16** (p.23) Rival publicity: advance the publicity counter when it gains publicity. The rival spends publicity to research techs (SOLO-RIVAL-06).
- **SOLO-17** (p.23) Rival data: data the rival gains goes directly into its computer; if the computer is full, into the data pool. The rival's data pool has no limit. When data is added to a marked computer space, the rival gains the benefit shown.
- **SOLO-18** (p.23) Rival techs do not give their normal abilities. The rival stores techs on its board and discards them to enhance actions (SOLO-RIVAL-04, -07, -08). Bonuses of a researched tech are still given, except it never gets the probe-launch or 2-data bonuses.
- **SOLO-19** (p.26, p.27) Rival passing: if the rival starts a turn with no cards left in its action deck, it passes: remove the top card from the end-of-round stack (the rival advances 1 on the progress track); if the rival was first to pass, rotate the solar system.
- **SOLO-21** (A-023, ruled) When the rival passes, its used action cards are shuffled back into its action deck.
- **SOLO-20** (p.24) The rival's turns follow SOLO-RIVAL-01. The rival has no hand, does not take free actions, and takes turns "as though they were the other player in a two-player game".

## End of the game

- **SOLO-END-01** (p.27) The game ends after 5 rounds. Victory points are scored as in multiplayer. The rival does not score gold scoring tiles, but scores 5 points for each uncompleted objective. If the player has more victory points than the rival, the player wins.
- **SOLO-ALIEN-01** ('Oumuamua sheet 6) When the rival resolves the signal-in-'Oumuamua's-sector icon, it always marks the signal on the 'Oumuamua tile. The rival also collects exofossils; it marks a space requiring exofossils only when it has enough of them, otherwise it treats the space as occupied.
- **SOLO-ALIEN-02** (Centaurians sheet 8) When the species is discovered, the rival places one message milestone token as a player would and takes all leftover tokens, which it uses with its alien action card (S.18). When the rival reaches its message milestone, it chooses the leftmost or rightmost available reward by the decision arrow. The rival marks spaces that require paying data only if it has a full computer and enough leftover data to pay.
- **SOLO-ALIEN-03** (Exertians sheet 10) The rival can draw and play Exertian cards only through its action card for this species (S.19). It ignores the Exertian milestones. When the species is discovered, the rival does not draw or play any cards, but it advances on the progress track once for each marker it placed on the discovery spaces. At the end of the game all rival Exertian cards count as successfully completed and the rival scores full points for them. Then danger is compared with the player in the usual way (ALIEN-EXE-07).
- **SOLO-ALIEN-04** The Mascamites and Anomalies sheets give no solo rules text; their rival behavior is on the action cards S.15 and S.16 (`solo-rival-cards.md`). Open: A-014.
