# Solo sandbox (PLAN.md D33)

Not part of the rulebook: a practice game in which one player can try cards and situations out. Requested by the owner on 2026-10-14.

- **SANDBOX-01** (D33) A game created with the option `sandbox` is a game for one player without the solo rival, with the setup of a one-player game (the neutral markers as for two players). It is never rated. The option is refused for other player counts. The sandbox actions are refused in any other game.
- **SANDBOX-02** (D33) The player can search and add any card to their hand: every base card (and the promo cards when the game has them) and the alien cards of the species that are face up. A card that is added leaves the pile it was in (deck, discard, card row, round piles, species deck). Exertian cards go to the Exertian cards, not to the hand (ALIEN-EXE-02).
- **SANDBOX-03** (D33) The player can add or remove credits, energy, data (the pool holds at most 6), publicity (at most 10) and, once 'Oumuamua is face up, exofossils. A resource never goes below 0. One action changes a resource by at most 50.
- **SANDBOX-04** (D33) The player can turn any of the three discs one step in either direction. Probes on the disc go with it. This gives nothing for probes that are pushed and does not count as a rotation of the game (the next disc to rotate stays the same).
- **SANDBOX-06** (D33) When the game is created, the player can choose the two face-down alien species (left and right slot) among the five base species (and, in a game with the expansion, Arkhos, Glyphids and Amoeba), or leave a slot to chance. A slot left to chance takes a species from the seeded random order that was not chosen. The species stay face down: they are discovered by play (ALIEN-01).
- **SANDBOX-05** (D33) The sandbox can be used on the player's turn between decisions. Its changes can be taken back like other moves (UNDO-01). The engine checks every sandbox action itself; the legal moves hold one `sandbox` entry that lists what is possible.
