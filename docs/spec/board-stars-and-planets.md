# Nearby stars and planetary board (confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-12)

Read from the art and cross-checked against the repo (`constant/sectorSetup.ts`, `constant/boardLayout.ts`). The owner confirmed all 24 items in the check page (marks in `data/board-check-stars-planets.json`). Data in `data/stars.json` and `data/planets.json`; generated by `tools/board/stars_planets.py`.

## Nearby stars (sector boards)

- **BOARD-20** The four sector boards each carry two stars and cover two neighbouring sectors. Together they give the 8 sectors of the board. The four boards are placed in a random order around the board at setup (the owner confirmed that the position of the sectors is randomized each game, from the seeded generator, A-004); each board keeps its own two stars together, in the order printed. The drawing convention for the table: "low" is the star nearer the 3 o'clock side when the board is shown as a quarter ring in the top-right quadrant.
- **BOARD-21** Data slot sizes (tokens per slot) are 4 to 6 per star; measured from the slot cutout (44 px per token) and equal to the repo.
- **BOARD-22** Printed on every sector board: each player who contributed at least 1 marker gains 1 publicity (SECTOR-12); a marker in the second position of the data slot scores 2 points (SECTOR-03).
- **BOARD-23** Winner rewards. Where a board prints a circle next to the star it is the reward for the first win of that sector; the capsule next to it is the reward for later wins. Where the board prints one capsule only, the same reward applies to every win. The table gives both.

## Planetary board

- **BOARD-30** Orbiting a planet (ORBIT-03): the first orbiter at a planet scores 3 VP (covers the space, ORBIT-04); every orbit gives the orbit rewards listed below.
- **BOARD-31** Landing on a planet (LAND-03): every lander gains the lander rewards; the first lander(s) also gain the first-lander data. Mars has two first-lander spaces (2 data and 1 data). There is no limit to the number of landers (LAND-04).
- **BOARD-32** Moons (LAND-05): each moon has a single space for one lander and gives its own reward. Mars's Phobos and Deimos share one moon space (owner-confirmed).

## Species discovery spaces on the planetary board

- **BOARD-40** The planetary board has two alien species slots, each with discovery spaces for the three trace kinds (pink, yellow, blue) and three overflow spaces below, one per color (SETUP-11, LIFE-01, A-022). Owner-confirmed. The left slot in the art gives 5 VP and 1 publicity per discovery space; the right slot gives 3 VP and 1 publicity per discovery space. Every overflow space gives 3 VP. Which slot holds the "1st" and which the "2nd" species (rival cards S.3 and S.4) is not shown in the art.
