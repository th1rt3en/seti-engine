# Space Agencies expansion (PLAN.md D19, D35)

Sources (kept out of the repository by the owner's instruction; cited by section): **R16** = `SE2 rulebook EN 16.pdf` (8 pages, October 2025), **A14** = `SE2 aliens aid EN 14.pdf` (6 pages), **P3** = `SETI promo 03 - Space Agencies.pdf`, all from czechgames.com. Card, organization and quick start data that exists only as pictures is in `expansion-data.md`, taken from the repository's card extraction (`tools/card-tracker/cards-extracted.json`) and **not yet checked against the physical cards** by the owner. Every gap is an entry in `ambiguities.md` (A-028 and following) or in `docs/asset-gaps.md`.

The expansion is optional. With it off, the base game is exactly as in the other spec files (EXP-01).

- **EXP-ORDER-01** (A-053, owner 2026-10-15) The effects of a card, an organization, a quick start card or a board space are resolved from left to right as printed. An effect that needs a choice is finished before the next one starts.

## Components and the options

- **EXP-01** (D19, D35) The game options are `expansion` (the cards, species and signal tokens of the expansion are in the game), `organizations` (needs `expansion`: the organization module with quick start cards and 4 rounds) and `neutral_quick_start` (needs `expansion` and 1 or 2 players, optional module of R16 "Adjustments for 1-2 players"). With `expansion` off the engine behaves exactly as without the expansion.
- **EXP-02** (R16 components) The expansion has 11 organizations, 21 quick start cards, 42 cards for the main deck, 3 Xenolab tiles, 1 Futurespan token, 10 signal tokens (their supply is unlimited), 4 rival action cards, 3 long-term objective tiles, and three alien species (Arkhos, Glyphids, Amoeba) with their boards, rule sheets and components.
- **EXP-03** (R16 "Using this expansion") These parts are used whenever `expansion` is on, whether or not organizations are used: (1) shuffle the 42 new cards into the main deck, so the deck has 180 cards (with the promo cards of D23, 182); (2) mix the 3 new species with the 5 original ones and choose 2 at random for the game as usual (SETUP-11); (3) signal tokens are in the game.
- **EXP-04** (R16) The expansion's cards carry the expansion icon. (UI: the card images have it; nothing to implement.)

## Signal tokens

- **EXP-SIG-01** (R16 p.8) A signal token is a resource that some new cards, species and organization incomes give. The supply is unlimited (if it runs out, use a substitute). A player keeps their signal tokens in front of them.
- **EXP-SIG-02** (R16 p.8) Using signal tokens: during a scan action (SCAN-01 to SCAN-05) the player may discard signal tokens. For each token discarded, they may discard 1 additional card from the card row and mark a signal in the matching sector (like SCAN-03). Only an action that includes a scan action allows this: a card that merely marks signals is not a scan. Because the card row is refilled after the whole scan action (SCAN-03, A-026), a player cannot use more than two signal tokens in a single scan action (the card row has three cards: one for the normal row step and two more).
- **EXP-SIG-03** (R16 p.8) Some organizations gain a signal token as part of their income (EXP-ORG-13).

## Organizations (the organization module)

- **EXP-ORG-01** (R16 p.4) Each player controls an organization with unique powers and an asymmetric starting setup and income. The organizations replace the starting income card of the base game.
- **EXP-ORG-02** (R16 p.4) Games with organizations are 4 rounds long: rounds 2, 3, 4 and 5 are played. (Not recommended with 5 rounds. Quick start cards without organizations are not recommended either, FAQ; with 4 rounds and without organizations use the starting income card instead of an organization.)
- **EXP-ORG-03** (R16 p.4, setup) Setup: (1) prepare the end-of-round cards for rounds 2, 3 and 4 only; (2) determine the starting player and set the starting scores by turn order as usual (SETUP-32); (3) set the publicity of every player to 0; (4) deal each player 2 random organizations, 3 random quick start cards and 4 random cards from the main deck.
- **EXP-ORG-04** (R16 p.4) Each player chooses 1 organization and 2 quick start cards and keeps all 4 main deck cards. Then all players simultaneously resolve their 2 quick start cards and gain all the starting resources and effects of their chosen organization. (In the engine: the choice is made by each player in turn; the resolution follows EXP-ORG-06 and EXP-ORG-07.) The unchosen organization and quick start cards return to the box.
- **EXP-ORG-05** (R16 p.4 first game tip) For a first game it is recommended to give every player one of the four organizations numbered 1 to 4 (bottom left corner of the card), 2 quick start cards offered instead of 3 and 4 cards from the main deck. Those are 1 Orbital Dynamics, 2 Fenwick Research Center, 3 Stratus Core and 4 Deep Sky Survey (A-030); the option `beginner_organizations` deals exactly these (one each, as many as there are players, in random order), 2 quick start cards (both kept) and 4 main deck cards.
- **EXP-ORG-06** (R16 p.5) Starting resources and effects: the starting resources printed at the top of the organization (credits, energy, cards, publicity, data, and for some a probe launch or other effect), then the income increases.
- **EXP-ORG-07** (R16 p.5) Income increases: most organizations let you tuck cards for income; gain the resource of each tucked card immediately. Income increases are resolved after taking all starting resources. The player chooses which cards to tuck from the 4 cards dealt in setup and any extra cards gained from the organization or the quick start cards. Multiple increases are resolved one by one, so a random card gained by one can be used for the next.
- **EXP-ORG-08** (R16 p.5) Income: the income of the organization (the credits, energy, cards, publicity, data and signal tokens printed on its right side) is **not** resolved at game start. It is resolved at the start of rounds 3, 4 and 5, which is when the round before it ends: as in the base game (ROUND-02) there is no income after the last round, and round 2, the first round played, starts without income. (How this combines with INCOME-01 to INCOME-04 and ROUND-02: A-031.)
- **EXP-ORG-09** (R16 p.5) Passive power: always in effect, as its text says.
- **EXP-ORG-10** (R16 p.5) Once per round ability: a free action, available once per round. When used, mark its space to remind you it has been used this round. When the player resolves their income (EXP-ORG-08) they remove the marker. (In the engine: every player's used markers are removed when the income of that player is resolved, at the start of rounds 3, 4 and 5; round 2 starts with nothing used.)
- **EXP-ORG-11** (R16 p.8 FAQ) Helion Assembly and Cosmos Strategy Group: their topmost abilities (marking reward spaces when a tech is developed or a card of a color is played) resemble triggerable missions but do not count as such. If one trigger fulfills both a triggerable mission and one of these abilities, both can be marked.
- **EXP-ORG-12** (R16 p.8 FAQ) Futurespan Institute: a player may have only one card prepared at a time. A prepared card is no longer considered to be in the hand and cannot be used for anything except being played for free when the Futurespan token has been reached. The once per round ability can only be used if the token is on the scoring track.
- **EXP-ORG-13** (R16 p.8; data) The eleven organizations, their starting resources, incomes and abilities are in `expansion-data.md` (owner check pending).

## Quick start cards

- **EXP-QS-01** (R16 p.5) The top half of a quick start card may instruct the player to place an orbiter, a trace or signals: mark those, but do not gain any effects or other benefits for doing so. Nothing placed this way completes a task or a mission, and nothing triggers (no milestones, no first-orbiter bonus, no data from the signal, no sector win).
- **EXP-QS-02** (R16 p.5) The bottom half gives starting resources, points and other effects, usually related to what was marked but not always. After resolving the cards they can be returned to the box, unless they were tucked for income.
- **EXP-QS-03** (R16 p.5, "adjustments for 1-2 players", optional module) In games of 1 or 2 players, before players choose an organization and quick start cards, reveal 4 quick start cards at random. Mark any orbiters, signals or traces shown in the top half of those cards with neutral color pieces. Ignore the bottom half. (Neutral orbiters count as a nonplayer orbiter on that planet, neutral traces as nonplayer markers on the species board, neutral signals as nonplayer markers in the sector.)
- **EXP-QS-04** (R16 p.6) Neutral signals: when resolving a completed sector that includes neutral markers, treat them as if they belonged to another player. There can be at most 2 neutral markers in a sector, so the neutral color can never win. In a situation where it has the second most markers, one neutral marker is left in the sector (SECTOR-12).

## Solo game with organizations

- **EXP-SOLO-01** (R16 p.6) Solo with the expansion uses the standard solo mode (SOLO-*) with changes. Set up as a 2-player game. The neutral quick start cards (EXP-QS-03) can be included. Choose a difficulty and prepare the game for solo as usual. The rival does not get an organization or quick start cards; they gain alternative benefits instead (EXP-SOLO-02 to EXP-SOLO-04).
- **EXP-SOLO-02** (R16 p.6) The new extra starting action card is shuffled into the rival's action deck. It reads: "Then (1 advance) for each (gold star) of your difficulty level" (the rival advances one for each gold star of the difficulty). (Card image: A-033.)
- **EXP-SOLO-03** (R16 p.6) The rival's starting publicity is 4.
- **EXP-SOLO-04** (R16 p.6) Objectives (ignore at difficulty 1 star): remove all level I objectives from the game; stack the level II and III objectives by difficulty as in the base solo rules, then reveal the top three tiles as usual (they will be three level II objectives); shuffle the 3 long-term objective tiles and reveal 2 at random (the third is not used). So 5 objectives are revealed at the start: 2 long-term and 3 level II.
- **EXP-SOLO-05** (R16 p.6) The player chooses their organization and quick start cards as in a multiplayer game, after seeing the neutral quick start cards and the 5 revealed objective tiles. The starting effects of organizations and quick start cards do not complete tasks on objectives.
- **EXP-SOLO-06** (R16 p.7) Long-term objective tiles differ from regular objectives: (1) no replacement: when all three tasks are completed the tile is removed from the game, so it cannot be spent to prevent rival advancement at the end of rounds; (2) penalty: at the end of rounds 2, 3 and 4 the rival advances (the penalty of the tile, the picture shows the advance symbol; A-034) for each uncompleted task still visible on any long-term objective tile. Uncompleted long-term objectives are not scored by the rival at the end of the game. Tasks follow the same trigger rule as standard ones: if one trigger could mark several tasks (even on different objectives), the player chooses only one.
- **EXP-SOLO-07** (R16 p.7) When the rival runs out of advanced action cards at a higher difficulty and should gain an extra card, they gain an advance instead (the symbol on the page; A-034).

## New alien species (A14)

The three species are mixed with the five originals (EXP-03). Their boards, rewards and cards are data (A-032: the board pictures are missing from the repository; the rules below are from A14).

### Arkhos

- **EXP-ARK-01** (A14 p.1) When discovered, shuffle the 9 exploration cards face down and set 4 aside. The remaining 5 form the exploration deck. Players who marked the three discovery spaces gain minor exploration rewards, 1 reward per marker (the rival too).
- **EXP-ARK-02** (A14 p.1) Minor exploration reward: reveal one card from the exploration deck for each discovery space marked by any player. That player gains the minor exploration reward on the card. Revealed cards go to a discard pile next to the deck.
- **EXP-ARK-03** (A14 p.1) Security cards: deal 3 security cards in front of each player, life trace side up: 1 random card with a pink trace, 1 with a blue trace and 1 with a yellow trace each (there are 4 sets of 3 cards). With fewer than 4 players, return the remaining cards to the box without looking at them.
- **EXP-ARK-04** (A14 p.2) Exploring the ship: effects on the Arkhos board and on the security cards gain rewards from exploration cards, of two kinds: "reveal the next exploration card and gain its minor reward" (a red shield symbol) and "reveal the next exploration card and gain its major reward" (a blue shield symbol). When the exploration deck runs out, shuffle all revealed exploration cards with the four set aside, form a new 5-card deck and put the remaining 4 aside again.
- **EXP-ARK-05** (A14 p.2) The Arkhos board has four security levels, level 0 to level 3. Initially only the top three spaces (level 0) can be marked. To reach deeper sections the player must breach the security cards they were given.
- **EXP-ARK-06** (A14 p.2) Breaching: when a player marks an overflow space below the Arkhos board, they can breach their security card of the corresponding trace color: take it into the hand. 1 breached card gives access to level 1, 2 breached cards give access to levels 1 and 2, all 3 give access to all spaces. Once a player has no security cards left dealt in front of them they can mark any space of the Arkhos board.
- **EXP-ARK-07** (A14 p.2) Using security cards: a security card in the hand counts toward the hand limit and can be used like a regular card. Discarding it for its free-action corner effect gives a minor exploration reward; playing it as a main action for 2 credits gives a major exploration reward. Discarding or playing it does not lose the access to the deeper levels.
- **EXP-ARK-08** (A14 p.2, solo) When Arkhos is discovered, the rival gains an advance for contributing and 3 security cards like a player. If the rival would gain a breach effect it gains an advance instead. The only way the rival breaches its security cards is by playing its alien action card. The rival ignores Arkhos board spaces it cannot access yet. When choosing where to mark a trace, if both species have equally low available spaces, the rival prefers Arkhos.

### Glyphids

- **EXP-GLY-01** (A14 p.3) When discovered, shuffle the species deck and deal cards to the players who marked the three discovery spaces, 1 card per marker. Keep the deck near the board and turn the top card face up beside it. Spaces with the alien-head icon let a player draw a Glyphids card from the top of the deck or take the face-up card and replace it from the deck. These cards can be tucked for income or discarded for their free action and count toward the hand limit (ALIEN-04, ALIEN-05).
- **EXP-GLY-02** (A14 p.3) The translation board is attached to the Glyphids board; the two strips are inserted a random side up in its cutouts. It is used to decipher glyphs by slotting in glyph tokens.
- **EXP-GLY-03** (A14 p.3) Glyph tokens (35, 7 kinds): shuffle all and place one face up on each of the 6 designated spaces of the Glyphids board, beside each of the 7 planets, on each of the 8 sectors and on each tech stack that still has its 2-point token on top. The remaining tokens form a face-down stack near the species board.
- **EXP-GLY-04** (A14 p.4) A player gains a glyph token by: orbiting or landing at a planet (or a moon) with a token; winning a sector with a token; developing a tech with a token; marking a space of the Glyphids board that contains a token. The player takes the token, and nobody can gain a token from that place any more. Exception: the top three spaces of the Glyphids board are replenished immediately; if the supply runs out, the space has no effect any more.
- **EXP-GLY-05** (A14 p.4) Deciphering: as a free action a player may place one of their glyph tokens in an empty slot of the translation board and gain the associated resource. Top-row slots can be filled only when both slots beneath them are filled. Once a glyph type has been deciphered (placed), no more glyphs of that type can be added to the board. Tokens of an already deciphered type can still be collected but not placed; they are kept for the end-of-game scoring.
- **EXP-GLY-06** (A14 p.4) Glyphs on cards: the icons on Glyphid cards do not give glyph tokens. As long as that glyph has been deciphered, the player gains the associated resource; if not, the icon has no effect.
- **EXP-GLY-07** (A14 p.4) End of game: unplaced glyph tokens score per set of different tokens: 1 / 3 / 5 / 8 / 11 / 15 / 20 points for 1 to 7 different tokens. All of a player's sets are scored.
- **EXP-GLY-08** (A14 p.4, solo) The rival gains glyph tokens like a player. A token that is not yet deciphered is placed at once on the translation board and its reward gained (upper slots preferred when possible, the decision arrow breaks ties). If the reward is a signal token (expansion symbol) it gains an advance instead. If any top space of the species board is empty, the rival ignores it. At game end the rival scores 3 points per glyph token, whatever the type.

### Amoeba

- **EXP-AMO-01** (A14 p.5) When discovered, shuffle the species deck and deal cards to the players who marked the three discovery spaces, 1 card per marker; the top card goes face up beside the deck; the alien-head spaces draw a card as for the other species (ALIEN-05). These cards can be tucked for income or discarded for their free action and count toward the hand limit.
- **EXP-AMO-02** (A14 p.5) Organelle tokens: shuffle the five organelle tokens and deal them face up on the marked spaces of the Amoeba board.
- **EXP-AMO-03** (A14 p.6) Marking a space of the Amoeba board works as usual, but in addition to what the space shows, the player gains all bonuses of the organelles in the section of the matching color.
- **EXP-AMO-04** (A14 p.6) Cards: many Amoeba cards show a single organelle icon: the player chooses a single organelle in the matching section and gains its reward.
- **EXP-AMO-05** (A14 p.6) Mutating: each time a player gains organelle bonuses, from marking a space or from a card, they then move all those organelles 1 space in the direction shown on the board: the outer organelles move clockwise, the inner one counter-clockwise. When all bonuses of a section are gained, all organelles that were present move simultaneously. If the target space is occupied, skip it and move the organelle to the next empty space.
- **EXP-AMO-06** (A14 p.6, solo) When selecting spaces to mark, the rival ignores Amoeba spaces with 1 or 0 organelle bonuses.

## Promo card

- **EXP-PROMO-01** (P3) The promo card "Space Agencies" (SETI promo 03): after playing the card, launch a probe and gain a signal token. In addition, a player who played it in a previous game gets one extra quick start card in their next game with organizations (they are dealt 4 and still choose 2). The effect transfers from one game to the next. (How the engine knows who played it before: A-035; the card itself is not implemented until the owner rules.)

## Implementation status (engine)

Implemented and tested (`services/engine/tests/test_expansion.py`, `test_expansion_cards.py`, `test_arkhos.py`, `test_glyphids.py`, `test_amoeba.py`): EXP-01 to EXP-03, EXP-SIG-01, EXP-SIG-02, EXP-ORG-02 to EXP-ORG-13 with all 11 organizations (A-036 to A-039, A-051, A-052), EXP-QS-01 to EXP-QS-04, the 42 main-deck cards (SA.1 to SA.42, one test each, A-050), Arkhos (EXP-ARK-01 to EXP-ARK-07), Glyphids (EXP-GLY-01 to EXP-GLY-07) with all 10 cards, and Amoeba (EXP-AMO-01 to EXP-AMO-05) with 9 of its 10 cards. The new species are only drawn in games with the expansion and without the solo rival.

Not yet implemented: the solo game with the expansion (EXP-SOLO-*, EXP-ARK-08, EXP-GLY-08, EXP-AMO-06: the rival does not know the new species or the organizations; A-033, A-034), the long-term objectives (A-034), Amoeba ET.20 (A-049), the promo card (A-035), and the web boards of the three new species (placing markers on their spaces; the pictures are in place).
