# Cards and missions

Source: base rulebook pp. 7, 15, 19, 20, 28. Each card's own text is card data (see `card-verification-summary.md`); this file holds the rules that apply to cards in general.

## Card anatomy

- **CARD-01** (p.7) A card has: a free action (top-left, discard at any time during the player's turn for the depicted benefit); a main action cost (paid only when the card is played as the main action); an effect (the white part, resolved when played as the main action); an optional mission (a condition and a reward); a sector color (upper right corner, used for scanning); an income type (the resource it becomes when tucked); and a flavor text with no game effect.
- **CARD-02** (p.15) When the effect includes other actions (launching a probe, scanning, and so on), the player performs the action without paying its standard cost.
- **CARD-03** (p.7) Using a card uses only one benefit: effect, free action, or income (TURN-03).
- **CARD-04** (p.15) The cost printed on a card is paid in credits unless the card names another resource (alien Centaurian cards cost energy: ALIEN-CEN-02).

## Missions

- **CARD-10** (p.15) Conditional missions state a condition and a reward. Playing the card for its effect keeps it face up in front of the player.
- **CARD-11** (p.15) At any time during their own turn, if the condition is met, the player may complete the mission as a free action: gain the reward and turn the card face down. If the condition is already met when the card is played, the player may complete it immediately. The player is never required to complete it as soon as the condition is met.
- **CARD-12** (p.15) Triggerable missions can be triggered by things the player does. When triggered, the player may cover the matching circle with one of their markers and gain the depicted bonus. Only things done after the mission was played can trigger it; the mission is in play from the moment its card is played, so its own effect and play cost can trigger it (A-019). Each circle can be covered only once. The player does not have to use the effect that triggered it.
- **CARD-13** (p.15) A triggerable mission can be triggered even outside the player's own turn (for example when the solar system pushes a probe onto a space).
- **CARD-14** (p.15) If one effect would trigger several rewards (on the same card or on different cards), the player may cover any one of those spaces but only one at a time; the effect must trigger again to cover another.
- **CARD-15** (p.15) When all spaces of a triggerable mission are covered it is completed automatically: it is turned face down.
- **CARD-16** (p.15) Completed missions are kept face down in front of the player for the rest of the game. They count for gold scoring tiles (END-10).
- **CARD-17** (p.15 example) A visit trigger ("when you visit Mercury or Venus") fires only on moving onto the space, not when the probe is already there.

## End-of-game scoring cards

- **CARD-20** (p.15) Cards with a gold box score points at the end of the game. After resolving the effect, the card stays face up in front of the player for the rest of the game.
- **CARD-21** (p.21) At the end of the game, each player scores the gold boxes of the end-of-game scoring cards they played (END-02).

## Discards, draws, hand

- **CARD-30** (p.15) After resolving, a played card goes face up on the discard pile unless it is a mission or an end-of-game scoring card (or an alien card of a species that says otherwise).
- **CARD-31** (p.19) Refilling the card row (RES-04). Drawing (RES-05).
- **CARD-32** (p.20) Alien cards follow the rules of regular cards in hand, except Exertian cards, which are not in hand (ALIEN-EXE-02).
- **CARD-33** (p.6) Tucking: a tucked card is under the starting income card for the rest of the game and no longer counts as a card in hand.

## Terminology (p.28)

- **CARD-40** "at a planet": a figure (lander or orbiter) on the planetary board in orbit of the planet, on the planet, or on one of its moons. It does not include probes still on the solar system board.
- **CARD-41** "signal in a sector": mark a signal; the card specifies where.
- **CARD-42** "Discard a card from the card row and mark a signal in a matching sector."
- **CARD-43** "Mark a signal matching this color."
- **CARD-44** "Win a sector of this color" / "a win in a sector": see SECTOR-20.
- **CARD-45** "Move within the same ring": a move that does not change the distance from the Sun.
- **CARD-46** "You have a life trace of a kind": a trace of that kind you have marked: count the player's colored markers on the species and discovery board slots, by the color of the space; overflow spaces have colors like the others (A-017, A-022).
- **CARD-47** "Discard a card for its signal": discard a card from hand, check its upper-right corner, and mark a signal in a matching sector.
- **CARD-48** "Visit a planet": move a probe onto the planet's space (MOVE-08).
