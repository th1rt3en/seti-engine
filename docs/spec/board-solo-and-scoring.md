# Solo boards, objectives and gold scoring tiles (draft, owner check in progress)

Read from `assets/rival-board-*.png`, `assets/solo-objective-*.png`, `assets/gold-scoring-tile-*.png` and rulebook pp. 21 to 23, 26, 27. Data in `data/solo-boards.json`, `data/objectives.json`, `data/gold-tiles.json`; generated by `tools/board/solo_scoring_data.py`.

## Rival boards

- **SOLO-DATA-10** There are four rival boards: 1-star and 2-star share one. Per difficulty: the objective stack (level I / II / III counts), the number of cards in the starting action deck (4 basic, plus 1 advanced from 3 stars up) and the starting space of the progress marker.
- **SOLO-DATA-11** The progress track is a ring of 12 spaces that also names the rival's preferred tech. Clockwise from the top: blue-dot, blue-link, blue-branch, blue-cross, pink-dot, pink-link, pink-branch, pink-cross, orange-dot, orange-link, orange-branch, orange-cross. Color is the tech type (orange probe, pink telescope, blue computer) and the glyph is the stack on the tech board (dot, link, branch, cross). The growth icon sits between orange-cross and blue-dot.
- **SOLO-DATA-12** Rival computer: 6 spaces; the 2nd gives 1 publicity and the 4th gives 4 progress (the picture shows 1 publicity and an orange progress arrow marked 4). The analyze slot at the right end gives the rival a blue life trace and 1 energy (which is 1 progress, SOLO-10).
- **SOLO-DATA-13** The objective table printed on the left of every board: after round 1, 2, 3, 4 spend 1, 2, 3, 4 completed objectives (each missing one gives the rival 3 progress); after round 5 each uncompleted objective gives the rival 5 VP.

| 1 star | rival-board-1-2-star | 4 basic | none | blue-dot |
| 2 star | rival-board-1-2-star | 4 basic | 2 / 3 / 5 | blue-dot |

## Gold scoring tiles (8 faces)

- **SOLO-DATA-30** Four double-sided tiles. Each has three score slots: the first player to choose it takes the highest, the second the middle, and the third and fourth share the lowest (wide slot). Players score the value under their marker for each set they have (see END-10 to END-17).

| Tile | Side | Values (1st, 2nd, 3rd+) | Scores for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tech | A | 11 / 8 / 5 | each set of three techs, one of each type |
| Tech | B | 7 / 5 / 3 | each pair of techs (any two) |
| Mission | A | 4 / 3 / 2 | each completed mission |
| Mission | B | 8 / 6 / 4 | each pair of completed missions or end-of-game scoring cards |
| Income | A | 11 / 8 / 5 | each set of three tucked income cards, one of each type (credit, energy, card) |
| Income | B | 5 / 4 / 3 | each tucked income card of the type you have most (credit or energy) |
| Other | A | 8 / 6 / 4 | each set of three life traces, one of each color (overflow markers count) |
| Other | B | 8 / 6 / 4 | each sector win paired with an orbiter or lander |
