# Main actions and free actions

Source: base rulebook pp. 7 to 19, 28. Costs are paid when the action is taken as a main action; when a card effect grants an action, the standard cost is not paid unless stated (CARD-02).

## Launch a probe

- **LAUNCH-01** (p.8) Cost: 2 credits. Take one of the player's figures and place it on the Earth space of the solar system board.
- **LAUNCH-02** (p.8) Probe limit: by default a player may have only 1 probe in space. A player at the limit cannot take the Launch action. "In space" means anywhere in the rings of the solar system board; figures on the planetary board are not "probes in space".
- **LAUNCH-03** (p.17) Probe tech PT#01 raises the limit to 2 probes in space; this already applies to the tech's own immediate bonus (a free probe launch).
- **LAUNCH-04** (p.28) A card that grants a launch and says to ignore the probe limit lets the player ignore it for that launch only.

## Movement

- **MOVE-01** (p.8) Each 1 movement lets the player move one of their probes into an adjacent space, not diagonally.
- **MOVE-02** (p.8) To leave a space with asteroids the player pays 1 additional movement. (Probe tech PT#03 removes this cost: p.17.)
- **MOVE-03** (p.9) The Sun is not a space and cannot be moved through.
- **MOVE-04** (p.9) Movement sources: spend 1 energy to gain 1 movement (free action, FREE-01); discard a card with the movement icon in its corner for its movement (free action, FREE-02); card effects; techs.
- **MOVE-05** (p.9) If a probe moves into a space with a publicity icon, the player gains 1 publicity. There is no publicity for visiting Earth. Which spaces carry a publicity icon is board data (BOARD-04).
- **MOVE-06** (p.9, p.28) Every time a probe visits (moves onto) a space with a publicity icon the owner gains publicity, including when the solar system pushes the probe onto it, even on another player's turn.
- **MOVE-07** (p.17) Probe tech PT#03: when the player moves to asteroids they gain 1 publicity.
- **MOVE-08** (p.28) "Visit a planet" means moving a probe onto the planet's space. A "when you visit" trigger does not fire for a probe already on that space when the card is played.
- **MOVE-09** (p.27, solo) Visiting asteroids and comets are events that can trigger missions and objectives (CARD-20, SOLO-OBJ-04).

## Orbit a planet

- **ORBIT-01** (p.10) Cost: 1 credit and 1 energy. Main action. The player's probe must be on a space with a planet other than Earth.
- **ORBIT-02** (p.10) Remove the figure from the solar system board and place it in the orbit of that planet on the planetary board. This no longer counts against the probe limit.
- **ORBIT-03** (p.10) Gain the bonuses shown above the planet (usually data, a signal and income, in the example).
- **ORBIT-04** (p.10) The first player to enter orbit around a planet also scores 3 points; cover the space with the figure to mark that. There is no limit to the number of orbiters at a planet.
- **ORBIT-05** (p.10) Turning a probe into an orbiter usually gives the "tuck a card for income" effect (the bonus shown above the planet).

## Land on a planet or moon

- **LAND-01** (p.11) Cost: 3 energy; 2 energy if there is already an orbiter at that planet (any player's). Main action. The probe must be on a space with a planet other than Earth.
- **LAND-02** (p.17) Probe tech PT#02 reduces the cost by 1: 1 energy if there is an orbiter, 2 if there is not.
- **LAND-03** (p.11) Remove the figure from the solar system board and place it on the planet on the planetary board. Gain the bonuses shown in the middle of the planet (always a life trace of the landing kind, plus points).
- **LAND-04** (p.11) The first player to land on a planet also receives data; cover the space with the figure. Mars has two such spaces: the first two players to land there each choose one to cover. There is no limit to landers on a planet.
- **LAND-05** (p.11, p.17) Moons: a player cannot land on a moon unless a card effect or probe tech PT#04 allows it. When landing on a moon the player gains only the rewards depicted at the moon, not the planet's landing rewards (A-016); each moon has space for only 1 lander. The cost is the same as landing on the planet (p.17).
- **LAND-06** (p.11) Landing is the primary way to earn life traces of the yellow kind (LIFE-01). The bonuses shown in the middle of a planet always include one.

## Scan nearby stars

- **SCAN-01** (p.12) Cost: 1 credit and 2 energy. Main action. The scan resolves the steps printed on the player board, in any order the player chooses.
- **SCAN-02** (p.12) Step 1: mark 1 signal in the sector of Earth (the sector the Earth space is currently in).
- **SCAN-03** (p.12) Step 2: discard 1 card from the card row and mark 1 signal in a sector matching the discarded card's sector color (upper-right corner of the card): one of the two sectors of that color. The card row is refilled after the whole scan is finished (p.19, p.25).
- **SCAN-04** (p.12, p.17) The player may activate any telescope techs they have, in any order, and must pay any additional cost to activate most of them (TECH-10).
- **SCAN-05** (p.12) A scan always marks at least 2 signals; with the right techs, up to 4.
- **SCAN-06** (p.12) Scanning is the primary way to earn life traces of the pink kind (LIFE-01), through the rewards of sectors and board spaces; the Scan action itself gives no trace.
- **SCAN-07** (p.12) Marking a signal in a sector follows SECTOR-01 to SECTOR-12.

## Analyze data

- **ANALYZE-01** (p.14) Cost: 1 energy. Main action. Allowed only if the top row of the player's computer is filled with data. Bottom-row spaces (from computer techs) may be filled or empty.
- **ANALYZE-02** (p.14, p.17) Discard every data token in the computer (all spaces, including bottom-row spaces). Data in the data pool is kept.
- **ANALYZE-03** (p.14) Then mark a blue-kind life trace for one of the two alien species (LIFE-01). Analyzing is the primary way to earn the blue kind.

## Play a card

- **PLAY-01** (p.15) Main action: play a card for its effect (the white part of the card). The cost is printed on the card (in credits, or in the resource the card names).
- **PLAY-02** (p.15) After resolving, place the card face up in the discard pile unless it is one of: a conditional mission, a triggerable mission, an end-of-game scoring card, or an alien card that stays (CARD-10 to CARD-30).

## Research a tech

- **TECHACT-01** (p.16) Cost: 6 publicity. Main action. If the player has fewer than 6 publicity, they cannot take it. (Certain cards can be discarded for publicity as a free action.)
- **TECHACT-02** (p.16) First rotate the solar system (ROTATE-01). Then choose a tech the player does not have yet and take the top tile of the chosen stack.
- **TECHACT-03** (p.16) If the player is the first to take a tech from that stack, discard the 2-point tile and score 2 points.
- **TECHACT-04** (p.16) Gain the tech's printed bonus immediately; flip the tile and place it on the player board.
- **TECHACT-05** (p.16) Probe and telescope techs go on their specific slots; a computer tech goes on any computer tech slot, even one that has a data token on it (the tech goes under the token; this is not placing data and does not score the 2-point space the token covers).
- **TECHACT-06** (p.16) A card effect that gives a tech of a given type: the choice is limited to that type, no publicity is paid, and the solar system is rotated. If the player already has all 4 techs of that type, ignore the tech part (the solar system is still rotated).
- **TECHACT-07** (p.17) A player cannot have the same tech twice.

## Rotating the solar system

- **ROTATE-01** (p.16) The solar system is rotated: whenever a tech is researched (main action or card effect), and when the first player to pass in a round passes (PASS-03).
- **ROTATE-02** (p.16) The rotation counter on the tech board says which disc to rotate: first time disc 1 (the top disc), second time disc 2 (disc 1 rotates with it), third time disc 3 (the other two discs rotate with it); then back to disc 1. Each rotation advances the counter.
- **ROTATE-03** (p.16) Probes rotate with the disc they sit on. Because of cutouts in the discs, a probe may not move even though the planets in its ring move; at other times a probe in a cutout is pushed into the next space by a rotating disc. Pushed movement is free; if that space gives publicity, the owner gains it immediately (even on another player's turn: p.28).
- **ROTATE-04** (p.16 and p.28) Rotation happens for the round's first passer at the end of that player's turn, before taking the end-of-round card (p.28), even in round 5.
- **ROTATE-05** The exact geometry (which spaces a disc moves, which spaces are cutouts, what "next space" means for a pushed probe) is board data; see BOARD-05 and open question A-007.

## Free actions

- **FREE-00** (FAQ Q4) A free action may interrupt a main action but may not interrupt another free action: it is resolved completely first (FAQ-04).
- **FREE-01** (p.9) Spend 1 energy to gain 1 movement.
- **FREE-02** (p.9, p.7) Discard a card for the free action in its top-left corner (movement, publicity, data and so on, as depicted).
- **FREE-03** (p.14) Place data: take 1 data from the data pool and place it in the leftmost available space of the computer; the space's effect resolves immediately. Possible only on the player's own turn.
- **FREE-04** (p.14) Computer spaces: covering the publicity space gives 1 publicity; covering the income space lets the player tuck a card (INCOME-01); spaces on computer techs give 2 points on the upper space and the depicted reward on the lower space (TECH-20).
- **FREE-05** (p.18) Resource exchange (RES-06).
- **FREE-06** (p.18) Buy a card: spend 3 publicity to take a card from the card row or from the top of the deck. Available whenever the player has at least 3 publicity (owner note, 2026-10-13: not only at publicity 3 to 5, where the board draws the box).
- **UNDO-01** (owner request, 2026-10-14; D31) A free trade for 1 credit or 1 energy (2 credits, 2 energy or 2 cards given) can be taken back until the player's next action; the state is exactly as it was before the trade. A trade for a card cannot be taken back (A-026 open).
- **FREE-07** (p.15) Complete a conditional mission (CARD-11).
- **FREE-08** (p.15) Mark a triggerable mission circle when triggered (CARD-12); this may even be outside the player's own turn (CARD-13).
- **FREE-10** (p.6) The "tuck a card for income" effect (INCOME-01).

## Life traces

- **LIFE-01** (p.11, p.20) A life trace has three kinds (shown by color and symbol): scanning (pink), landing (yellow), analyzing (blue), plus a universal (wildcard) trace that stands for any of the three. The player marks a matching discovery space below an alien board if one is free; otherwise the overflow space of the matching color below, scoring 3 points (each overflow space has a color, A-022). Marking an overflow space is always allowed even if another space on an alien board is free (p.20).
- **LIFE-02** (p.20) Overflow markers count for "traces marked for an alien species" (card terms and gold tiles), as do markers on the discovery spaces and on the alien board itself.
- **LIFE-03** (p.20) Discovery spaces: a marker placed on a discovery space gives the reward printed there (for example 5 points and 1 publicity for the first, and further benefits when the species is discovered later: p.11).
- **LIFE-04** (p.20) Further research: once a species is discovered, each life trace gained can be marked in any empty space of the matching color on the alien board, and the reward is gained.
- **LIFE-05** (p.20) Nonplayer markers (games with fewer than 4 players) can only occupy the six discovery spaces under the alien boards; they never block a space on the species board itself.
