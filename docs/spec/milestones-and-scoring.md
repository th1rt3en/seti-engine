# Milestones and scoring

Source: base rulebook pp. 18, 20, 21, 28.

## Milestones

- **MILE-01** (p.18) A milestone triggers when a player's score counter reaches or passes the milestone's score. It is resolved between turns, after the current player has finished their free actions (not immediately). It is resolved at the end of the turn in which it was reached or passed.
- **MILE-02** (p.18) Gold milestones are at 25, 50 and 70 points. When a player reaches or passes one, at the end of that turn they choose one of the gold scoring tiles and mark it with one of their markers.
- **MILE-03** (p.18, p.21) Each gold scoring tile has several spaces worth different amounts. The first player to choose a tile places a marker on its highest value; the second player gets the second-highest value, and so on. There is room for everyone to choose the same tile. A player cannot choose the same tile twice, so a player who reached 70 points has a marker on 3 of the 4 tiles.
- **MILE-04** (p.18) A player who scores beyond 100 may pass these spaces again, but they have no effect on the second trip around the scoring track.
- **MILE-05** (p.5, p.18) Neutral milestones (games with fewer than 4 players) are at 20 and 30 points. At the end of a turn in which a player reaches or passes one, a nonplayer research group discovers something about one of the two alien species. Each species has 3 discovery spaces: if any is empty, move a nonplayer marker from the milestone to the leftmost empty space. This can lead to the discovery of a species (ALIEN-01).
- **MILE-06** (p.18) If all six discovery spaces are already full, both species have been discovered and neutral milestones have no effect. Neutral milestones also have no effect if all of their nonplayer markers have been used.
- **MILE-07** (p.18) 4 players: neutral milestones are ignored.
- **MILE-10** (p.18) If several players reached milestones in the same turn, they resolve in order starting with the player whose turn just ended and proceeding clockwise. The neutral milestone is resolved last.
- **MILE-11** (p.20) A species discovery resulting from milestones is resolved after the milestones (TURN-04).

## End of game

- **END-01** (p.21) The game ends after round 5. Once the last player has passed, final scoring: end-of-game scoring cards, plus gold scoring tiles, plus any alien species scoring step (for example Exertian danger). The player with the most points wins. If scores are equal, the players tie.
- **END-02** (p.15, p.21) End-of-game scoring cards: the player scores each gold box of the cards they played (card data).
- **END-03** (p.21 and Exertian sheet) Alien species may add one more scoring step (ALIEN-EXE-07). It is applied after all other points have been scored.

## Gold scoring tiles

Each tile is double sided. A player scores, for each set they have made, the value under their marker on that tile. A player has a marker on a tile only if they chose it at a gold milestone (MILE-02). Tile values by position (highest first) are tile data: the values below were read from the tile art and are to be verified by the owner.

- **END-10** (p.21) Tech, side A (11/8/5): for each set of three techs, one of each type. Count the techs of each type; score for the number of the type you have the least of.
- **END-11** (p.21) Tech, side B (7/5/3): for each pair of techs (any two). Five techs make 2 pairs.
- **END-12** (p.21) Mission, side A (4/3/2): for each completed mission. Conditional and triggerable missions both count.
- **END-13** (p.21) Mission, side B (8/6/4): for each pair of completed missions or end-of-game scoring cards. A pair is 2 completed missions, 2 end-of-game scoring cards, or 1 of each.
- **END-14** (p.21) Income, side A (11/8/5): for each set of three cards tucked for income, one of each income type (credit, energy, card). The resources printed on the starting income card (3 credits, 2 energy, 1 random card) are not counted.
- **END-15** (p.21) Income, side B (5/4/3): score for the income type you have tucked the most: credits or energy. The starting income card's printed resources are not counted.
- **END-16** (p.21) Other, side A (8/6/4): for each set of three life traces marked, one of each color. Markers on the overflow spaces count by the color of their space (A-022).
- **END-17** (p.21) Other, side B (8/6/4): for each sector win paired with an orbiter or lander. That means the total number of wins or the total number of orbiters and landers, whichever is smaller. When counting sector wins, every marker beside a star counts, even several in the same sector.
