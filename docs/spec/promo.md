# Promo cards SE EN 01 and SE EN 02 (PLAN.md D23)

Source: the owner's rulings in A-001, the official FAQ (FAQ-C300) and the card data (`docs/spec/data/card-definitions.json`). The two cards are a game setting, separate from the Space Agencies expansion (D19, D22).

- **PROMO-01** (A-001, D23) The setting "include the promo cards" is chosen when the game is created, shown to all players, and allowed in rated games. Off (the default), the main deck is the 138 base cards. On, SE EN 01 and SE EN 02 are shuffled into the main deck (140 cards) and are drawn and played like any other card.
- **PROMO-02** (A-001, FAQ-C300) SE EN 01 "Not a planet since 2006" (cost 0, free action 1 movement, income card, red sector): when played, it stays in front of its player and Pluto becomes a planet for the rest of the game that only that player can use. Pluto has no space on the solar system board and cannot be visited. A probe of the owner on any space of the outermost ring (the ring of Uranus and Neptune) can use an Orbit action or a Land action to become an orbiter or lander at Pluto, moving from the board to the card. The costs are the normal ones: Orbit 1 credit and 1 energy; Land 3 energy, or 2 energy if the owner has an orbiter at Pluto, less with the landing tech. Pluto has 1 orbiter space and 1 lander space. Orbiting gives 11 points, 3 publicity and 1 trace of any color. Landing gives 11 points, 4 data and 1 yellow trace. There is no first-orbiter or first-lander bonus. Pluto counts as a planet for other effects: its orbiter and lander count for missions that need an orbiter or a lander and for the gold scoring tile with orbiters and landers; cards that remove an orbiter or a lander (#15, #84) can remove them so that Pluto can be used again. The free orbit and land of cards such as #ET.3 are not offered for Pluto.
- **PROMO-03** (A-001, A-027) SE EN 02 "Gateway to Mars" (cost 1 credit, free action 1 data, income credit, blue sector) is a triggerable mission with two circles. Each time the owner orbits or lands at Mars (or its moons), or plays a card that names Mars in its flavor text, they may cover a circle: the first gives 2 publicity, the second 5 points (CARD-12). The cards whose flavor text names Mars are #13, #14, #22, #34, #76, #84, #85, #119 and ET.4 (A-027).
