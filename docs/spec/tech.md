# Technologies

Source: base rulebook pp. 5, 16, 17. Researching a tech is TECHACT-01 to TECHACT-07 (`actions.md`).

- **TECH-01** (p.5, p.16) There are 12 technologies: 4 probe techs (PT#01 to PT#04), 4 telescope techs (TT#01 to TT#04) and 4 computer techs (CT#01 to CT#04). Each has 4 tiles in its stack. A player cannot have the same technology more than once (p.17).
- **TECH-02** (p.16) Each tech tile has an immediate bonus printed on it. The bonuses are tile data: they are listed from the tile art and must be verified by the owner. The labels in `assets/MANIFEST.md` for the tech tile images are marked "probable".

## Probe techs (always-on)

- **TECH-PT01** (p.17) Probe limit is 2 (LAUNCH-03). Immediate bonus: launch a probe for free.
- **TECH-PT02** (p.17) Landing costs 1 less energy (LAND-02).
- **TECH-PT03** (p.17) Gain 1 publicity when moving onto asteroids. Leaving asteroids no longer costs 1 extra movement (MOVE-02, MOVE-07).
- **TECH-PT04** (p.17) The player may land on a planet's moon instead of the planet; the cost is the same (LAND-05).

## Telescope techs (only during a Scan action)

- **TECH-10** (p.12, p.17) Telescope techs can be used only when performing a scan action. They may be activated in any order. Most need an extra payment.
- **TECH-TT01** (p.17) During a scan, mark a signal in a sector adjacent to Earth's sector instead of marking Earth's sector. Immediate bonus: 2 data.
- **TECH-TT02** (p.17) During a scan, discard one card from hand to mark an extra signal in a matching sector (the sector color of the discarded card).
- **TECH-TT03** (p.17) During a scan, pay 1 publicity to mark an extra signal in a sector with Mercury.
- **TECH-TT04** (p.17) During a scan, pay 1 energy to launch a probe, or gain 1 movement. The launch costs only that 1 energy; the 2 credits of the Launch action are not paid (FAQ-22).

## Computer techs

- **TECH-20** (p.17) A computer tech can be placed in any computer tech slot. Its upper space gives 2 points when data is placed there. Its lower space gives the depicted reward (CT#01: 1 credit, CT#02: 1 energy, CT#03: 1 card, CT#04: 2 publicity).
- **TECH-21** (p.17) Data can be placed in a lower space only if the space above it is filled. Data in the upper spaces is placed left to right. Lower spaces are not placed in order and need not be filled before analyzing.
- **TECH-22** (p.17) During Analyze, data is cleared from all spaces (ANALYZE-02).
- **TECH-23** (p.16) A computer tech placed over an existing data token does not count as placing data (TECHACT-05).
